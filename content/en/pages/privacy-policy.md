## Data processed

### Contact form

When you use the contact form, the following data is sent by email: your name, your email address, the subject and the content of your message. It is not stored in the site's database.

- **Purpose:** answering your request and, where relevant, discussing a project.
- **Legal basis:** the legitimate interest in answering people who get in touch, or pre-contractual steps taken at your request (GDPR article 6.1.b and 6.1.f).
- **Retention:** [[for example: 3 years after the last exchange]], unless a contract follows.

### Server logs

Like any website, the server records technical logs (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser) to keep the site secure and working.

- **Legal basis:** the legitimate interest in securing the site.
- **Retention:** [[period applied by the host, for example 12 months at most]].

## Cookies

This site uses **no analytics, advertising or social media cookies**, and loads no external resources (fonts, scripts, videos).

A strictly necessary session cookie may be set in two cases: to display the confirmation message after sending the contact form, and when logging in to the administration area. These cookies are exempt from consent (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act), so no consent banner is needed.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, write to [[contact email address]].

If, after getting in touch, you consider that your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
